A coach you talk to, that remembers what matters to you

Squire.Coach lives in a side panel in Chrome. You talk to it out loud. It helps you plan tomorrow, keeps your projects and open items straight, gives you a short daily lesson, and picks up where you left off yesterday.

What it does

Plans your day with you

A two-minute conversation each evening turns everything on your mind into a short, real plan. You approve it; Coach never fills your day on its own.

Remembers, so you don't repeat yourself

What you tell it is kept in your own record. You can read every word, change it, or delete it.

Keeps your open items

Things you owe, by project. Coach can suggest one; it only becomes yours when you accept it.

A short lesson each day

Aimed at what you're working on, with a video when one fits.

Your record, your say

  • Nothing about you changes without your OK. Every change to what Coach knows about you shows up first on screen, to approve, edit or skip.
  • A receipt for every change. Each saved change is recorded with the date and what changed.
  • Two ways to keep it: in your own private GitHub repository with your own keys, or in a Squire account (beta) that you sign in to with Google.
  • No ads, and your information is never sold.

The details, including every outside service your words pass through, are in the privacy policy. The rules for using Squire.Coach are in the terms of service.
