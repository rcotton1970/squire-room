Privacy policy

Last updated October 13, 2026 · Squire.Coach, run by Rick Cotton · rick@squire.coach

The short version: what Coach knows about you lives in a record you can read, change and delete. Nothing in it changes without your OK. To work, your words pass through a few outside services, listed below. There are no ads, and we never sell your information.

Two ways to use Coach

With your own GitHub repository

Your record is kept in a private GitHub repository you own, and you use your own keys for Claude and the voice. We don't have a copy.

With a Squire account (beta)

You sign in with Google. Your record is kept in our database (Supabase, United States), separated so only your account can read it.

What Coach keeps

  • Your record: what you've told Coach about yourself, your goals, projects and open items, your daily plans, your profile read and your reactions to it, notes and reminders, and a log of each conversation.
  • Voice notes: the written transcript. The audio is kept only if you turn on "Keep the audio of my voice notes".
  • Receipts: each change to your record is recorded with its date and what changed. In GitHub mode, the receipt is the commit in your repository.
  • Squire accounts only: your Google name and email, your settings, and how many requests you made each day (to keep within limits and costs). If you choose to share your answers to lesson check-ins, they are stored without your name or your words, only as anonymous counts.
  • In this browser: your settings and any keys you entered stay in Chrome's storage for the extension on this computer. They're never sent to us.

What Coach never does

  • Change what it knows about you without showing you first and getting your OK. (Conversation logs and other activity logs are written as you go so nothing is lost; you can still see and delete them.)
  • Show ads, sell your information, or share it with anyone for marketing.
  • Record keystrokes, passwords, or what you type into web pages.

Each of these companies handles data under its own privacy policy and terms.

Watch (off unless you turn it on)

If you turn on Watch, it notes which sites you're on and for how long, so Coach can tell which of your goals you've finished. That activity stays on your computer and is deleted after 7 days. It never includes incognito windows, sites on your block list, keystrokes or anything you type. If you also turn on matching, short summaries (site names and minutes, never web addresses) go to Claude about once an hour, and each send gets a receipt. You can pause it, export it, or delete it at any time.

Invited reviewers

If someone invites you to give feedback on a page like a logo board or a practice room, you sign in with Google. Your answers and the time you submitted are stored with your name for the person who invited you. Other reviewers can't see your answers until they've submitted their own.

Keeping and deleting

  • GitHub mode: your record is your repository. Delete files or the whole repository whenever you like.
  • Squire accounts: email us to delete your account and everything in it. Watch data has its own Delete button, with 30 days to change your mind.
  • Keys in this browser: Settings has "Forget my keys".

Security

Squire account data is separated by account in the database, so one account can't read another's. Connections are encrypted. No system is perfect; if something goes wrong that affects your data, we'll tell you.

Children

Squire.Coach is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in Coach before it takes effect.
